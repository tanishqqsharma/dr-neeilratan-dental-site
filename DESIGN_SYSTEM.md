# Design System Extracted from the Inspiration Mockup

## Typography
The image does not contain font metadata, so these are close visual matches:

- Headings: **Manrope**, weights 700–800, tight line-height and negative letter spacing.
- Body/navigation: **Inter**, weights 400–700.
- Type scale: 12px labels, 13–15px navigation/support copy, 16–18px body, 32–54px section headings, 48–84px hero heading.

## Component patterns
- Rounded pill buttons, usually solid blue or white with a thin blue outline.
- White cards with 18–28px radii, cool grey-blue borders, and soft navy shadows.
- Thin blue outline icons inside pale-blue rounded squares/circles.
- Floating trust, experience, and promise cards overlapping photography.
- Deep navy CTA banners with electric-blue/cyan glow effects.
- Repeating section rhythm of roughly 72–112px vertical spacing.

## Layout
- Header: logo left, compact centered navigation, pill appointment CTA right.
- Hero: two-column layout with oversized heading on the left and clinical photography on the right; floating cards overlap the image.
- Feature strip: four equal icon-box columns in a raised white panel.
- Content sections: two-column doctor/about blocks, six-card service grid, technology cards, horizontal review carousel, and map/hours split layout.
- Mobile: stacked sections, collapsible nav, two-column cards collapsing to one column, and horizontally scrollable reviews.
