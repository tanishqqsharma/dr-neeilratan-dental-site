SERVICE 01

Dental Implants

A durable, natural-looking way to replace missing teeth and restore confident function.

Dental implants replace a missing tooth root with a biocompatible foundation that supports a crown, bridge, or denture. Dr Neeilratan Chauhan evaluates bone support, bite, medical history, and the final restoration before recommending treatment.

Key benefits

  • Natural appearance and stable chewing
  • Helps preserve surrounding bone
  • Does not rely on adjacent healthy teeth
  • Planned with implantology-focused care

Often considered for

  • One or more missing teeth
  • Healthy gums and suitable bone support
Ask about Dental Implants

SERVICE 02

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Relieves pain and saves a deeply infected or damaged natural tooth whenever possible.

Root canal treatment removes infected tissue from inside the tooth, carefully cleans and shapes the canals, and seals them to reduce the risk of reinfection. An Apex Locator and Endomotor support accurate, controlled endodontic treatment.

Key benefits

  • Aims to preserve the natural tooth
  • Relieves infection-related discomfort
  • Modern instruments improve precision
  • Usually followed by a protective crown

Often considered for

  • Teeth with deep decay or trauma
  • Lingering sensitivity or swelling
  • Infection confirmed during examination
Ask about Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

SERVICE 03

Dentures

Custom removable replacements designed to restore appearance, speech, and everyday eating.

Complete and partial dentures are planned around the patient’s mouth, remaining teeth, bite, and comfort. Follow-up adjustments help improve fit as the tissues adapt.

Key benefits

  • Restores multiple missing teeth
  • Supports facial appearance and speech
  • Customised for comfort and fit
  • Options for complete or partial tooth loss

Often considered for

  • Several missing teeth or a full arch
  • Patients considering a removable restoration
Ask about Dentures

SERVICE 04

Braces

Orthodontic treatment to improve alignment, bite, hygiene access, and smile confidence.

Braces apply controlled forces to guide teeth into improved positions over time. Treatment planning considers crowding, spacing, jaw relationship, oral hygiene, and realistic treatment duration.

Key benefits

  • Improves tooth alignment and bite
  • Can make daily cleaning easier
  • Treatment progress is monitored regularly
  • Personalised plan for each smile

Often considered for

  • Children, teenagers, and adults with crowding
  • Spacing, rotated teeth, or bite concerns

Types of braces

  • Metal brackets
  • Ceramic
  • Aligners (Clear)
Ask about Braces

SERVICE 05

Scaling & Cleaning

Professional removal of plaque and hardened deposits for healthier gums and fresher breath.

Scaling removes deposits that normal brushing cannot remove. The visit may include polishing and personalised guidance on brushing, interdental cleaning, and maintenance frequency.

Key benefits

  • Supports gum health
  • Helps reduce bad breath and staining
  • Prevents buildup from progressing
  • Comfort-focused preventive care

Often considered for

  • Routine preventive care
  • Bleeding gums or visible deposits
  • Stains or persistent bad breath
Ask about Scaling & Cleaning

SERVICE 06

Crowns & Bridges

Fixed restorations that protect weakened teeth or replace missing teeth with a balanced result.

A crown covers and strengthens a damaged tooth, while a bridge replaces a missing tooth using carefully planned support. Shade, bite, gum health, and the condition of supporting teeth are assessed before treatment.

Key benefits

  • Protects weakened or root-treated teeth
  • Restores shape, function, and appearance
  • Fixed alternative for selected missing teeth
  • Planned for a comfortable bite

Often considered for

  • Damaged, heavily filled, or root-treated teeth
  • Fractured or missing teeth that need fixed restoration

Types of crowns

  • Metal crown [CAD/CAM]
  • Metal fused ceramic crowns
  • Zirconia crowns
  • DMLS crowns
Ask about Crowns & Bridges

SERVICE 07

Smile designing

Personalised options to improve the appearance and balance of your smile.

Smile designing considers tooth shape, spacing, gum appearance, and your goals. Recommendations are planned after a clinical examination.

Key benefits

  • Personalised planning around your smile goals
  • Options to address tooth shape and spacing
  • Considers gum appearance and overall balance

Often considered for

  • People considering changes to their smile
  • Teeth space closing
  • Gum & frenum corrective surgery
  • Tooth gems (Dental jewellery)

Types

  • Direct/Indirect veneers
Ask about Smile designing

Cosmetic dentistry (Dental jewellery)

Cosmetic options to personalise your smile, discussed with your dentist before treatment.

Available options depend on your oral health, the tooth, and your preferences. Your dentist can explain expected results, care, and suitability during a consultation.

Key benefits

  • A personalised cosmetic option for your smile
  • Planned with attention to oral health and suitability

Often considered for
